LEGACY RESULTS - AFFECTED BY DATA LEAKAGE
=========================================

DRSE_ManualAlpha_TestResults.xlsx and the eleven "sentiment distribution
alpha *.png" figures came from the pre-canonical 3-way pipeline
("Experiment #1 3 way split V1.py"), which made its own train/test partition
while loading branch checkpoints that had been trained on a different, unrelated
partition of the same rows.

The alpha sweep in that workbook peaks at accuracy 0.7236 (alpha = 0.8). That
figure is contaminated. On the canonical split the same family of models scores
around 0.62-0.63.

The SHAPE of the alpha curve is still informative - performance rises steeply to
about alpha 0.5-0.6 and then flattens, which is the qualitative finding that the
CNN-BERT branch carries most of the signal. That observation survives. The
absolute numbers do not.

For a citable alpha analysis on the canonical split, use instead:

    ../alpha_grid_search_results.xlsx
    ../result 4 way V2/alpha_grid_search_results.xlsx
    ../../improvement/figures/alpha_curve_with_ci.png   (with confidence intervals)
    ../../improvement/Chapter4_Final_Tables.xlsx        (sheet "T5 CV Selection")

See ../../README.md for the full explanation of the two eras, and
../../RESULTS_CONSOLIDATED.xlsx sheet "04 Legacy (do not cite)".
